# Technical Debt & Refactoring Analysis: Workout Challenge

## Executive Summary
The workout-challenge project shows generally good code organization with modern SvelteKit architecture. However, there are significant opportunities for complexity reduction, TypeScript improvements, and elimination of code duplication. The most critical issues are in the large visualization components (`ProgressChart.svelte` and `AnalyticsGraph.svelte`) which exceed 1,000 lines each.
